# Support

Questions: [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/srinitude/webular/discussions). Bugs: a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/srinitude/webular/issues) with the command, the input, and what you expected. Security flaws: report them privately, never in public.

- **Security problems:** follow the steps on the [Security](/security) page.
- **Conduct concerns:** follow the [code of conduct](https://github.com/srinitude/webular/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Read the [docs](/docs) first. To fix something yourself, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/srinitude/webular/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
