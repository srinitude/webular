# Support

- **Questions and ideas:** ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/srinitude/webular/discussions).
- **Bugs and feature requests:** open an [issue](https://github.com/srinitude/webular/issues). Include the command, the input, and what you expected.
- **Security problems:** do not post them in public. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Conduct concerns:** follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Read [README.md](README.md) and the [docs](docs) first. To fix something yourself, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
