## Intent

- "What do you want this for?" Options: a named goal from the folder, a fix, something to add, your own words.

## Outcome

- "When this is done, what is there that is not there now?" Options: a file, a working feature, a decision, your own words.

## Criteria

- "How will we know it is done?" Options: a check passes, a person signs off, a number is reached, your own words.

## Proof

- "What would you look at to believe it?" Options: test output, a screenshot, a live link, your own words.

## Boundaries

- "What must stay as it is?" Options: named files or features, a budget, a deadline, nothing.

## Method

- "Which way should we build it?" Options: two or three ways that fit every earlier answer, your own words.
